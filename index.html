About Us

Our Mission & Purposes

West Wing Residences Homeowners' Association, Inc. is a non-stock, non-profit homeowners' association duly organized under Republic Act No. 9904.

  1. Promote and protect members' mutual interests and assist in community development;
  2. Construct, manage, maintain, and operate adequate facilities and services;
  3. Organize socio-cultural projects to awaken community consciousness and belonging;
  4. Promote quality of life through livelihood projects and economic activities;
  5. Support local government in delivering services and implementing policies;
  6. Ensure safety and security; contract security and maintenance services;
  7. Adopt rules for use of common areas; impose penalties for violations;
  8. Maintain, repair, sanitize, and beautify common areas;
  9. Contract administrative, legal, accounting, and professional services;
  10. Operate recreation facilities; use income to meet operating expenses;
  11. Discharge any lien or encumbrance upon the subdivision;
  12. Levy and collect fees, dues, and assessments;
  13. Exercise all powers necessary to accomplish the foregoing purposes.

Association By-Laws

📋 Article I — Membership

  • Automatic Membership: All homeowners automatically become members.
  • Membership Fee: ₱500.00 one-time
  • Monthly Dues: ₱29.00 per square meter
  • Delinquency: 3 unpaid months → rights suspended. Grace period: 60 days.

💰 Article II — Dues & Fees

TypeRate
Membership Fee₱500.00 (one-time)
Monthly Dues₱29.00 / sq.m.

🗓️ Article III — Meetings

  • Annual Meeting: 3rd week of November each year
  • Quorum: Majority of members in good standing
  • Proxy: Allowed in writing; valid up to 3 years

🏛️ Article IV — Board of Directors

  • Board Size: 5 members
  • Term: 2 years
  • Officers: President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, Auditor
